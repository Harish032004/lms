Safe and effective anxiety control for young patients

Course Overview

This comprehensive offline course equips dental professionals with the knowledge and skills to safely administer sedation and manage pain in pediatric patients. Learn pharmacological and non-pharmacological techniques, emergency protocols, and hands-on sedation monitoring at our institute.

Hands-on Sedation Training
Emergency Protocols
Certification Included
10 Seats Only

Course Curriculum

5 modules
  • Patient assessment and selection criteria
  • ASA classification in children
  • Informed consent and documentation
  • NPO guidelines and pre-sedation preparation
  • Pediatric airway anatomy and considerations
  • Nitrous oxide/oxygen inhalation sedation
  • Oral sedatives: dosing and administration
  • Monitoring during minimal sedation
  • Complications and management
  • Hands-on nitrous oxide workshop
  • Pharmacology of sedative agents
  • Ketamine, propofol, and midazolam
  • Intravenous sedation techniques
  • Monitoring and equipment
  • Reversal agents and recovery
  • Recognition of adverse events
  • Airway obstruction and management
  • Laryngospasm and bronchospasm
  • Cardiovascular emergencies
  • Emergency drug and equipment review
  • Local anesthesia techniques for children
  • Computer-assisted delivery systems
  • Post-operative pain control
  • Non-pharmacological pain management
  • Case-based discussions

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on sedation monitoring with simulation mannequins
  • Learn from leading pediatric anesthesiologists
  • Master emergency response protocols
  • Certification in Pediatric Sedation
  • Small batch for personalized instruction
